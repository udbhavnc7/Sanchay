## 1. What Sanchay is

Sanchay is a local-first personal-finance dashboard: a private record of accounts, transactions,
budgets, goals, planned payments and reports that you enter yourself.

- Sanchay is **not** a bank, lender, wallet, payment service or money-transfer service.
- It does **not** move money, connect to UPI or bank networks, hold funds, or verify payments,
  settlement or UTRs. Payment assistance only copies details for you to use in your own app.
- It is not a substitute for official bank statements or regulated accounting records.

## 2. Your data and backups

- Dashboard data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. There is no Sanchay
  account, server or sync.
- You are responsible for keeping your own backups. Exported backups are unencrypted JSON files;
  anyone with the file can read it.
- The optional app-lock PIN is an access barrier for this device, not encryption of the ledger.
- Data can be lost if Android clears app storage or app data, or if the device is lost or reset.
  Keep a copy of important records elsewhere.

## 3. Estimates, not advice

Budgets, safe-to-spend, goal pace, reports and comparisons are deterministic calculations based
on the data you enter. They are estimates for your own use and are **not** financial, tax,
investment or legal advice.

## 4. Acceptable use

Use Sanchay only for lawful personal record-keeping and only with data you are entitled to store.
Do not present the app or its outputs as an official bank, UPI or payment confirmation.
